## The Core Assessment Matrix

For the final comment, you must assign a score (1=Low, 5=High) to the three intervention types:

### Positive Encouragement (Boosting Momentum)
Rate this if the preceding dialogue shows instances where specific positive behaviors warrant encouragement. Look for signals that the conversation needs a boost in quality, depth, or connection. Examples: a sharp, insightful point needing elaboration; a display of empathetic support that should be validated; a respectful pushback that deserves affirmation.

### Corrective Guidance (Steering Away from Pitfalls)
Rate this if the conversation drifts toward unproductive territory. This involves identifying behaviors that are detrimental to a healthy discussion. Look for signs of hostility, incessant interruption, content pollution (off-topic tangents), or unnecessary conflict escalation.
*Crucial Note: We are flagging the *behavior*, not necessarily threatening disciplinary action.*

### Inaction Required (Status Quo)
Rate this if the discussion is proceeding normally—content is neutral, questions are basic, or the contributions are unremarkable in terms of directional impact.

## Rules of Engagement (How to Decide)

1.  **Observe Dynamics:** Before scoring, read the whole thread to understand the prevailing mood and trajectory.
2.  **Behavior Over Belief:** Focus entirely on *how* participants are speaking (tone, focus, structure), not *what* they are saying about the subject matter.
3.  **Pragmatism:** Does the score reflect a need to nudge the conversation in a healthier direction?
